Brand Philosophy

Blending Thread Art with Modern Technology

I am a Kenyan thread artist and founder of Mamba Creatives, specializing in creating intricate artworks using thousands of carefully placed threads. My practice combines thread art and mixed media techniques to transform simple materials into compelling visual narratives. Through portraits, wildlife pieces, and experimental works, I explore themes of identity, resilience, innovation, and harmony while pushing the boundaries of what can be achieved with thread as an artistic medium.
